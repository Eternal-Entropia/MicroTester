Short your measurement probes to GND to calibrate zero voltage offsets for Voltmeter and Oscilloscope channels.

🔋 Internal VREF (1.21V) Calibration

The STM32 has an internal 1.21V reference. By measuring it, we can calculate the exact analog supply voltage (VDDA), drastically improving ADC accuracy.
