• Capacitance (C): 1 pF .. 10,000 µF
• Equivalent Series Resistance (ESR)
Diodes & LEDs
• Forward Voltage Drop (Vf)

• Inductance (L): 10 µH .. 1000 H
• DC Resistance (Rdc), Q Factor & Reactance (XL)
Please discharge capacitors!
Always ensure all capacitors are fully discharged before connecting them to probe pins (TP1, TP2, TP3) to protect your microcontroller from high voltage damage.

🔬 Component Tester Calibration

Short all three measurement probes (TP1, TP2, TP3) together and click Calibrate. This measures the resistance of your wires (Zero Resistance offset) to improve measurement accuracy for small resistors.
